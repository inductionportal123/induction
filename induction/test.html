Important Instructions

Read the following points carefully before submission of form:

  • Read the eligibility criteria published in the newspaper carefully and fill the form for the relevant post.
  • Fill the application form properly with complete and correct information.
  • Name, Father Name, and Date of Birth must be filled as per matriculation certificate.
  • Enter SEQ number/account number before uploading challan.
  • Upload challan form along with computerized receipts issued by the bank. Otherwise, the application will be rejected.
  • After successfully uploading the challan, click on the Undertaking and Submit button to final submission of the application.
  • Once the application is submitted, your profile will be locked, and no further changes can be made.
  • Intimation regarding written test/interview will be uploaded on the induction portal.
  • Frequently log in to your account to check the status of your application.
  • Before final submission of the form, make sure your data is accurate.
  • After successful submission of the application, the account will be locked, and no further changes can be made.
  • FGEI Dte will not be responsible for any wrong information/entry by the candidate, and no fee will be refunded.
